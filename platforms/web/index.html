FM-1 Midi-Mania Studio

Play the FM-1 in your browser, and load your own MIDI files as songs.

Z–' Q–O keys 1–= FX … REC [ ] OCT ↑ ↓ SELECT ← → ALGORITHM Shift-click holds a button

Quick start
  1. Press Switch on, then SEQ 0 for SONGS. Pick a song with SELECT ↑ ↓ and your part (KEYS, DRUMS, GUITAR, BASS) with ALGORITHM ← →.
  2. Choose the instruments: ENV 3 opens SOUNDS, a knob for each part (KNOB 1 KEYS, 2 DRUMS, 3 GUITAR, 4 BASS). Turn one quickly to see its whole list. ENV again goes back.
  3. In SONGS, KNOB 1 picks PLAY to play your part yourself and be scored (CPU: the FM‑1 plays it). KNOB 3 changes the layout, KNOB 4 how many notes are yours.
  4. Press PLAY -. Play the keys Z–' Q–O as the notes reach the line. PLAY again stops; HOME 7 returns to the game.

Turn a knob with the mouse wheel or by dragging it. Your own MIDI files: drop them on this page (Your songs).

Your songs

Switch on the FM-1 first. Your songs load into the FM-1 on this page, so it needs to be on to add MIDI files.

Drop MIDI files anywhere on this page. Each is set up for you (its piano, drums, guitar and bass go to the FM-1's four parts); press Load and it's in SONGS (the SEQ button), after the built-in songs. Advanced lets you choose which instrument plays what. Your songs stay in this browser; have an FM-1? Send them to it.

Notes each part keeps: KEYS C2–C7 (61 keys), GUITAR E2–E6, BASS E1–G4 (C4 is middle C). Notes outside are left out, and the card says how many. DRUMS keeps every hit on channel 10.

    Not connected
