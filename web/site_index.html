Midi-Mania

A keyboard rhythm game firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: songs from MIDI files, notes falling to the keys, the FM-1 playing the backing.

Midi-Mania Studio

Play the FM-1 in your browser, and load your own MIDI files as songs. No FM-1 needed.

Install on your FM-1

Put Midi-Mania on your FM-1 over USB, from Chrome or Edge. The official firmware can be put back the same way.
