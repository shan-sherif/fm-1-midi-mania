FM‑1 Midi-Mania

A keyboard rhythm game for the M-VAVE FM‑1. Turn your MIDI files into songs, watch the notes fall to the keys, and play your part while the FM‑1 plays the rest.

Open Studio: try it in your browser, load songs into your FM‑1 Install on your FM‑1

No FM‑1 needed: in your browser, play the built-in songs or load your own MIDI files and play them right there. Free and open source.
Have an FM‑1? The same page sends your songs to it over USB.

See it

Your MIDI, onto your FM‑1

Any MIDI file can be a song. Drop it on Studio, see it as a piano roll, and it's set up for you: the parts are chosen automatically, so you can send it to your FM‑1 over USB straight away, all from the browser. Want more control? The advanced options are there too.

Open Studio

What it does

Your songs, from MIDI

Drop your own MIDI files on FM‑1 Midi-Mania Studio and play them right there in your browser, no FM‑1 needed. Each is turned into a song with drums, keys, bass and guitar fitted to the FM‑1's sounds. Happy with it? Connect your FM‑1 and send it over from the same page.

Play your part

Pick keys, drums, guitar or bass. Its notes fall to a line on the screen, laid out like the FM‑1's keys, while the FM‑1 plays the rest of the song on its own synth engines.

Scored as you play

Every note is judged PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD or MISS, with a combo, a score and your result at the end. Your best for each song is kept, and a quick calibration makes the timing fair.

Songs on your FM‑1

Send up to 16 songs* to your FM‑1 over USB, delete the ones you're done with, and keep a backup of them on your computer.

Get started

  1. Try it in Studio The FM‑1 in your browser, running the same code as the instrument. Play the built-in songs or drop in your own MIDI files.
  2. Install it on your FM‑1 From Chrome or Edge, over USB. The official firmware goes back on the same page, with a backup of your songs and settings first.
  3. Send your songs In Studio, connect your FM‑1 and send the songs you tried. Find them in SONGS (the SEQ button).
  4. Play SEQ opens SONGS: pick a song, and your part with ALGORITHM. ENV chooses the instruments, a knob for each part. PLAY starts the song; play the keys as the notes reach the line.

* Up to 16 songs, as long as they fit: the FM‑1 keeps your songs in 240 KB of memory, so how many fit depends on their size. A song's size comes from its notes: each note takes 12 bytes (each beat another 4), and a song takes whole 4 KB blocks. So a longer song, a busier one, or one with more parts playing takes more room. A short song like the built-in FM Keys (about 300 notes) takes 4 KB, so 16 of those fit easily; a busy four-minute song with all four parts (around 3,000 notes) takes about 40 KB, and six of those fill the memory. Studio shows each song's size and the room left on your FM‑1.
